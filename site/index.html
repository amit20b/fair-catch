How values work. Every player and pick gets a 0–10,000 value (10,000 = the #1 player), blended from full trade-value boards updated Oct 16. Redraft 1QB: FantasyCalc trade market for 1QB / 14-team / PPR (45%), the FantasyPros Week 6 trade value chart (30%) and Roto Street Journal's Week 6 chart (25%). Redraft Superflex: FantasyCalc superflex market (60%) and FantasyPros rest-of-season consensus (40%). Dynasty: the full FantasyCalc dynasty market (55%) and FantasyPros dynasty consensus (45%). Players on season-ending IR are removed from Redraft. Settings then reshape the values: scoring shifts RB/WR/TE, TE premium lifts tight ends, league size changes the curve, Contender adds this-season production, Rebuild rewards youth and picks.
Star bonus. Two good players are worth less than one star with the same total, because one lineup spot holds one player. The side receiving the best player gets extra credit. Within 5% is a fair trade.
Sources: FantasyCalc, FantasyPros trade value chart, FantasyPros rankings (via DynastyProcess), Roto Street Journal, Sharp Football IR tracker.
