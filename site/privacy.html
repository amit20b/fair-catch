Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version: we keep your email and a scrambled copy of your password so you can sign in. Your leagues, rosters and settings stay on your own device. We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies. To count visitors, we use a random number that does not identify you.

Who we are

Fair Catch is an independent fantasy football trade calculator. In this policy, "we" means the people who run Fair Catch. You can reach us at support@faircatch.com.

What we store on our server

Only if you create an account:

  • Your email address.
  • A scrambled copy of your password (a salted PBKDF2 hash). We cannot read your password, and we never see it in plain text after you type it.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email. We do not get your Google password, contacts or anything else.
  • Account details: when you joined, and until when you have Pro.
  • Sign-in sessions: a scrambled copy of each sign-in token, so we can tell it is you. Sessions end after 60 days or when you sign out.
  • Free Pro month: if you got one of the free months, we keep a one-way scrambled form of your email so the same email cannot claim it twice. It cannot be turned back into your email.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, address or payment card.

Visit counter

To know how many people use Fair Catch, the app keeps a random number on your device and, while the app is open, tells our server about once a minute that this number is active. We store that number with the times it was seen, and, if you are signed in, which account it belongs to. It holds no name, IP address, location or device details.

What stays on your device

These are kept in your browser's own storage and are not sent to our server: the leagues you connect, your Sleeper username, your settings (format, scoring, league size), trades you build, your Pro license key, and your Yahoo sign-in tokens. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Trade from a screenshot reads the picture on your device. The image is not uploaded anywhere.

Services Fair Catch talks to

  • Sleeper: when you connect a Sleeper league, your browser reads public league data (rosters, teams, settings) straight from Sleeper.
  • Yahoo (when available): you sign in on Yahoo's own page and allow read-only access. Our server passes your request to Yahoo and back. It does not keep your Yahoo tokens or league data.
  • Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", for sign-in only. The app also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • Lemon Squeezy: when you buy Pro, payment happens on Lemon Squeezy's checkout, and the app checks your license key with them. We never see your card details. Their privacy policy covers what they collect.
  • Cloudflare runs our account server, and GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, they process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and keep it safe.

How we use it

To sign you in, keep your account safe, give you the Pro features you have, stop abuse (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts), and answer you if you write to us. That is all.

Deleting your data

Open Account in the app and tap Delete account. Your account and its sign-in sessions are deleted right away. Only the scrambled free-month record above remains, so the offer cannot be claimed twice. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and sign-in attempts are rate limited. No system is perfect, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

Fair Catch is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time, by emailing support@faircatch.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app.
